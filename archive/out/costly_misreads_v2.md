## 1. The client deck

**Contrast:** A funny mistake story versus a real work emergency.

**If C misreads it:** If C laughs along when A is panicking, C makes the mistake feel humiliating instead of helping contain it.

### Version 1 — Funny office blunder

Laugh is doing: A is offering the mistake as a ridiculous story for the group to enjoy.

C should: Join the amusement and invite the aftermath of the story.

- A: [annoyed] I sent the client the deck with our internal comments.
- B: [surprised] The one that says, "make this less boring"?
- A: [big laugh] And three slides were titled, "blah."
- C: [laughs] Amazing. Please frame their reply when it comes in.

### Version 2 — Panicked work mistake

Laugh is doing: A is nervously covering genuine fear about the consequences.

C should: Move immediately into practical support, not teasing.

- A: [annoyed] I sent the client the deck with our internal comments.
- B: [surprised] The one that says, "make this less boring"?
- A: [worried] [nervous laugh] And three slides were titled, "blah."
