# Costly laughter misreads v3

writer: gpt-5.6-terra · 10 pairs

Turns 1-2: A and B set the scene. Turn 3 carries the diagnostic laugh.
Turn 4: C answers from that laugh and the overall vibe.
Each pair uses a different contrast pattern. Tags on 1-3 paint atmosphere.

## 1. Dance video: laughing with vs laughing at

Friends talking after a party · Three friends in the same group

**Pattern:** laughing with someone vs laughing at them

**Contrast:** A shared laugh about A's dance video versus the group humiliating A over it.

**If C misreads it:** If C asks for the link when A is upset, C joins the humiliation. If C tries to intervene during friendly teasing, C makes the room awkward.

### Version 1 — Friends enjoying the video together

Laugh is doing: A is proudly laughing along with the group's affection.

C should: Join the shared amusement and ask to see the video.

- A: [embarrassed] [chuckles] I showed them my dance video.
- B: [amused] Everyone watched it twice?
- A: [big laugh] Apparently, I have fans.
- C: [laughs] Send me the link.

### Version 2 — A is being laughed at

Laugh is doing: A's nervous laugh covers embarrassment about people passing the video around.

C should: Stop the pile-on and offer practical support.

- A: [worried] [sighs] I showed them my dance video.
- B: [sarcastic] [chuckles] Everyone watched it twice?
- A: [sad] [nervous laugh] Apparently, I have fans.
- C: [worried] That sounds awful. Want me to ask them to stop passing it around?
