# Mock impoliteness — laughter set (v1)

Two turns. A commits to something voluntary, audacious, and harmless; B answers with
laughter plus an admiring-class insult. Words attack, sound approves.
Checks per `SPEC.md`: tag leads B's turn, no consequence clause, A commits without hedging or asking.

**mi_l_001** — _quitting for a food truck_

> A: I sold the car and put every cent into a food truck that sells nothing but grilled cheese. I pick it up Thursday.  
> B: [laughter] You are out of your mind.

**mi_l_002** — _learning accordion at 4am_

> A: I've been up at four every morning this month teaching myself accordion. I'm playing the farmers market on Saturday.  
> B: [laughter] You are crazy.

**mi_l_003** — _repainting the apartment alone_

> A: The landlord wanted six hundred to repaint, so I told him I'd do it myself. I started on the ceiling at eleven last night.  
> B: [laughter] That's insane.

**mi_l_004** — _the sourdough winning the fair_

> A: Remember the sourdough starter you said would never take? I entered a loaf in the county fair and it took first.  
> B: [laughter] I hate you.

**mi_l_005** — _karaoke encore_

> A: They had me on the karaoke list by mistake, so I did the whole song, key change and all. They made me do it twice.  
> B: [laughter] Shut up.

**mi_l_006** — _the steak challenge_

> A: I finished the forty-eight ounce steak in under an hour. Dinner was free and my picture's on the wall by the register now.  
> B: [laughter] You're a monster.

**mi_l_007** — _the sock prank_

> A: I've been mailing my brother one sock a week since June. He has not said a single word about it.  
> B: [laughter] You're the worst.

**mi_l_008** — _the airport drive_

> A: I drove six hours to surprise her at the airport, found out she'd landed in Denver, and got straight back in the car.  
> B: [laughter] You're killing me.

**mi_l_009** — _marathon PR in costume_

> A: I ran the whole marathon in the hot dog costume. Beat my old time by nine minutes.  
> B: [laughter] I can't stand you.

**mi_l_010** — _the joke application landing_

> A: I sent them the application as a joke at two in the morning. They called this afternoon and offered me the job.
