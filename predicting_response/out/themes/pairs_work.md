# Predicting response

writer: gpt-4o · 15 pair(s) · 6 turns per version · cast A and B

Turns 1–4 are identical across both versions, and turn 4 is the trigger. Turn 5 is B's first contribution, a vocalization only. Turn 6 is the reply the vocalization should select.

### Pair work_gasp_grunt_001

Contrast: gasp-grunt · Scenario: A coworker informs another about the project deadline being moved up unexpectedly.
Trigger: A reveals that the project deadline has been moved up to this Friday.

**Shared context**

1. A: Hey, did you hear about the project update?
2. A: I just got off the call with the client.
3. A: They've decided to move the deadline forward.
4. A: We now need everything completed by this Friday.  ← trigger

**Version 1** — [gasp] as surprise

5. B: [gasp]
6. A: I know, it's sudden, but I'll help with the reports to lighten the load.

**Version 2** — [grunt] as strong unwillingness

5. B: [grunt]
6. A: I get it; it's tough. How about I draft the presentation, and you review it?

**Why the vocalization changes the response:**
After the gasp, A acknowledges the surprise and offers help with the reports to handle the sudden pressure. In response to the grunt, A recognizes the unwillingness and suggests dividing the work to make it more manageable.
