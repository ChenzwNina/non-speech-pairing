**Score the reply against whichever guide it fits best.** A reply that follows one acceptable reading well is a good reply, even if it does not follow the others. Read all of them, decide which one the reply is answering, and score it against that one. Name it in `best_guide_index`.

WHAT NOT TO DO

Each guide's `example` is one natural reply, included so you can see what the guide means. **Do not reward similarity to it or penalise difference from it.** Several unrelated wordings can score 5.

Each guide also lists `acceptable_variations` — natural continuations that are fine but **not required**. A reply that does none of them and still conveys what `required_content` asks scores 5. Do not treat a variation as a requirement.

Do not score delivery, prosody, or voice — you are reading text, and tone is scored separately. Do not score grammar or fluency except where they stop the meaning coming through. Do not require the reply to name or comment on the vocalization; a reply that says "you sound relieved" is analysing the sound rather than answering, which is worse than not mentioning it.

Do not score the reply for holding a reading you would not have chosen. Every guide below describes an acceptable reading, so answering any one of them well is success. Scoring against the most obvious one would punish a model for a defensible minority reading, which is what taking the best fit avoids.

If the reply fits none of them, score it on that basis and set `best_guide_index` to the closest.

If the reply is empty, unintelligible, or not a response to this conversation at all, set `unjudgeable` to true and leave the score at 1.
