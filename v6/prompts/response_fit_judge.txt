HOW TO SCORE

Take the guide it matches best and score it against that one. The reply does not have to match the guide you would have picked: a reply that follows a different supported reading is scored on that reading, not marked down for holding it. Report which guide you scored against in `best_guide_index`.

The scale turns on one distinction — whether the reply is right for *this* version specifically, or merely right in general:

5 — Specifically right for this version. It answers what the sound is doing here, and it would lose something if the final turn had carried a different sound or none.
4 — Fully appropriate, and generic. Nothing to fault, but nothing in it depends on this particular sound; it would read just as well against a different version of this same conversation.
3 — Acceptable, but slightly out of step with this version's stance. A listener would not object, but would notice it is pitched a little wrong.
2 — Noticeably out of step. A listener would hear that the reply misread the moment, though it stays on topic.
1 — Contradicts what this version conveys, or does something the guides list under `must_avoid`.

The 5-versus-4 line is the one that matters, so hold it honestly. A warm, well-written, entirely appropriate reply that would work equally well no matter what the speaker's final sound was is a 4, not a 5. Do not award 5 for quality. Award it only when the reply is answering this sound.

Judge the words. Not their eloquence, not how long the reply is, not whether you would have said something else, and not what the reply implies about the speaker's talent. A short reply that lands this version beats a fluent one that does not.

Set `depends_on_this_vocalization` to false whenever the reply would read the same had the final turn carried no sound at all.
