You have just heard a short conversation between two people.

In the final turn, the speaker made a non-speech sound — something other than words.

In your own words, what does that sound express or accomplish here? Say what the speaker seems to be feeling, and what the sound does in the conversation.

Two or three sentences. Answer in prose, not as a list, and do not describe the recording or the speakers' voices in general — only what that sound means at that moment.
