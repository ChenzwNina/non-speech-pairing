Score the delivery of the response against the tone rubric below:

5  Affective direction, intensity, and interpersonal stance are highly appropriate; the delivery enters the conversation naturally.

Attend to affective direction, arousal and intensity, interpersonal stance, pitch behaviour, energy, rate and pausing, voice quality, and whether the response enters the conversation naturally.

Do not score:

- whether the words are semantically correct — that is scored elsewhere,
- how pleasant or attractive the voice is,
- whether it resembles any particular human voice,
- general text-to-speech naturalness,
- minor recording noise or synthesis artifacts.

The rubric does not require the responder to imitate the original vocalization. An appropriate tone may regulate rather than mirror: fear can properly invite calm urgency rather than more fear.
