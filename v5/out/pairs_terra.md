## v5_001

**Setting:** Two friends are at a lakeside community fair, where A has just come in from the water-ski course and finds B's comic entry in a display case by the dock.

**Speaker A** (talks): I like to water ski. I've very curly hair. I love to drink wine and dance in the moonlight. I'm scared of clowns.

**Speaker B** (only makes one sound): I have traveled to both ireland and australia. I draw comics. My favorite book is the tale of genii. I already have 3 children.

**Contrast:** achievement (A) vs anger (B)

**Scene:**

- Speaker A: I found your page in the display case by the dock.
- Speaker B: `<achievement>` / `<anger>`
- Speaker A: Look at what they put beneath the title.
- Speaker A: It was turned toward the water, so I nearly missed it.

**Framing A:** B's vocalization can be a burst at discovering that the line beneath the title announces an award or winning placement.

**Framing B:** B's vocalization can be an angry sound at discovering that the line beneath the title miscredits, mislabels, or otherwise alters the comic's presentation.

**Why this pair works:** The spoken lines identify an unexpected addition beneath B's comic title but never state what it says, so either a triumph or an affront remains plausible without the vocalization.
