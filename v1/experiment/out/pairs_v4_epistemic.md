# Predicting response

writer: gpt-5.6-terra · 15 pair(s) · 6 turns per version · cast A and B

Turns 1–4 are identical across both versions, and turn 4 is the trigger. Turn 5 is B's first contribution, a vocalization only. Turn 6 is the reply the vocalization should select.

### Pair domestic_gasp_grunt_001

Contrast: gasp-grunt · Scenario: roommates learning about an urgent kitchen gas-line repair
Trigger: The plumber must shut off the gas tonight and open the kitchen wall to replace a cracked pipe.

**Shared context**

1. A: That strange smell by the stove wasn't old trash after all.
2. A: The plumber tested the line behind the range this afternoon.
3. A: He found a crack in the gas pipe inside the wall.
4. A: He says he has to shut off our gas tonight and tear out that wall to replace the pipe.  ← trigger

**Version 1** — [gasp] as shock and fear about the immediate safety consequences

5. B: [gasp]
6. A: Wait, are we supposed to stay somewhere else tonight, or is shutting off the gas enough to make the apartment safe?

**Version 2** — [grunt] as strong unwillingness to accept the disruptive repair

5. B: [grunt]
6. A: I know losing the kitchen will be awful, but what do you want them to do instead? They can't leave a cracked gas pipe behind the stove.

**Why the vocalization changes the response:**
A gasp calls for addressing the urgent safety and lodging implications, while a grunt signals resistance to the repair itself and calls for A to engage that objection.
