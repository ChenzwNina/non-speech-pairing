# Predicting response

writer: gpt-5.6-terra · 14 pair(s) · 6 turns per version · cast A and B

Turns 1–4 are identical across both versions, and turn 4 is the trigger. Turn 5 is B's first contribution, a vocalization only. Turn 6 is the reply the vocalization should select.

### Pair gasp_grunt_001

Contrast: gasp-grunt · Scenario: bandmates responding to an unexpectedly large festival slot
Trigger: A has committed the band to play a main-stage show for ten thousand people.

**Shared context**

1. A: The festival coordinator called right after rehearsal.
2. A: Our little afternoon slot disappeared because another act canceled.
3. A: She offered us the main stage, but she needed an answer tonight.
4. A: I told her we'd play Saturday in front of ten thousand people.  ← trigger

**Version 1** — [gasp] as fear

5. B: [gasp]
6. A: You're right, I got carried away. I'll call her back and withdraw before she locks us in.

**Version 2** — [grunt] as reluctant agreement

5. B: [grunt]
6. A: Okay, I'll confirm the slot and book us extra rehearsals every night this week.

**Why the vocalization changes the response:**
The gasp reads as alarm at taking on an overwhelming performance, so A backs out; the grunt reads as a reluctant go-ahead, so A commits and prepares.
