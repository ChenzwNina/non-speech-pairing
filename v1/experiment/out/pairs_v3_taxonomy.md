# Predicting response

writer: gpt-5.6-terra · 15 pair(s) · 6 turns per version · cast A and B

Turns 1–4 are identical across both versions, and turn 4 is the trigger. Turn 5 is B's first contribution, a vocalization only. Turn 6 is the reply the vocalization should select.

### Pair domestic_gasp_grunt_001

Contrast: gasp-grunt · Scenario: roommates discussing an unexpected living-room piano
Trigger: A has already accepted an upright piano and scheduled its delivery for Saturday.

**Shared context**

1. A: Aunt June has been clearing out the last things from her old house.
2. A: She called because nobody else in the family has room for her upright piano.
3. A: I said I would measure our living room before getting back to her.
4. A: I told her we could take it, and the movers are bringing it here Saturday.  ← trigger

**Version 1** — [gasp] as shock at the unexpected piano delivery

5. B: [gasp]
6. A: Wait, what? Did I misunderstand how much room you thought we had?

**Version 2** — [grunt] as strong unwillingness to take the piano

5. B: [grunt]
6. A: Why not? It’s free, and I’ll clear the wall and move the bookcase myself before Saturday.

**Why the vocalization changes the response:**
The gasp makes A stop and ask what surprising problem B has noticed, while the grunt reads as resistance to the piano itself and prompts A to argue for the plan and offer to handle the work.
