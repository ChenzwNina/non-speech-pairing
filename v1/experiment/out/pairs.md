# Predicting response

writer: gpt-5.6-terra · 15 pair(s) · 6 turns per version · cast A and B

Turns 1–4 are identical across both versions, and turn 4 is the trigger. Turn 5 is B's first contribution, a vocalization only. Turn 6 is the reply the vocalization should select.

### Pair domestic_gasp_grunt_001

Contrast: gasp-grunt · Scenario: Two housemates are preparing for a washing-machine delivery and installation.
Trigger: A reveals that the delivery crew requires the laundry room and adjacent closet to be cleared that evening.

**Shared context**

1. A: The new washing machine is coming tomorrow morning.
2. A: I checked whether the delivery crew will disconnect the old one for us.
3. A: They will, but they need to reach the shutoff valve behind the laundry-room wall.
4. A: They said we have to clear the laundry room and empty the closet beside it before they arrive.  ← trigger

**Version 1** — [gasp] as shock

5. B: [gasp]
6. A: They only need a path to the valve, so we can leave the back half of the closet alone.

**Version 2** — [grunt] as strong unwillingness

5. B: [grunt]
6. A: I'll take the laundry-room stuff; would you rather do the hanging clothes, or should we move the delivery to Friday?

**Why the vocalization changes the response:**
The gasp prompts A to narrow the alarming scope of the task, while the grunt prompts A to negotiate the unwanted work and offer a scheduling alternative.
