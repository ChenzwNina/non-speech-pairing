# Predicting response

writer: gpt-5.6-terra · 10 pair(s) · 6 turns per version · cast A and B

Turns 1–4 are identical across both versions, and turn 4 is the trigger. Turn 5 is B's first contribution, a vocalization only. Turn 6 is the reply the vocalization should select.

### Pair domestic_gasp_laughter_001

Contrast: gasp-laughter · Scenario: roommates discovering what the robot vacuum picked up
Trigger: The robot vacuum is dragging a pair of underwear around the living room.

**Shared context**

1. A: I kept hearing the robot vacuum bumping into the coffee table.
2. A: I thought the cat had knocked one of its brushes loose again.
3. A: Then I saw it coming out from under the sofa with something caught underneath.
4. A: It has one of your red underwear tangled in the brush and is dragging it around the living room.  ← trigger

**Version 1** — [gasp] as shock

5. B: [gasp]
6. A: Oh no, I didn't realize it had grabbed anything. I'll switch it off and get them out before the brush ruins them.

**Version 2** — [laughter] as amusement

5. B: [laughter]
6. A: I know, it looks absurd. I'll take one picture of its little victory lap before I rescue them.

**Why the vocalization changes the response:**
A gasp makes the tangled clothing seem alarming and calls for immediately stopping the vacuum, while laughter treats the scene as funny and invites A to play along briefly before dealing with it.
