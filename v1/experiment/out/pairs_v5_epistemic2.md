# Predicting response

writer: gpt-5.6-terra · 15 pair(s) · 6 turns per version · cast A and B

Turns 1–4 are identical across both versions, and turn 4 is the trigger. Turn 5 is B's first contribution, a vocalization only. Turn 6 is the reply the vocalization should select.

### Pair domestic_gasp_grunt_001

Contrast: gasp-grunt · Scenario: roommates considering a free kitchen cabinet from a neighbor
Trigger: The free cabinet weighs three hundred pounds and must be carried upstairs today.

**Shared context**

1. A: Mrs. Bell downstairs is replacing the cabinet in her kitchen.
2. A: She said we can have the old one instead of her paying to haul it away.
3. A: It would finally give us somewhere to keep the pots that are piled on the counter.
4. A: But it weighs three hundred pounds, and she needs it carried up our narrow stairwell today.  ← trigger

**Version 1** — [gasp] as shock

5. B: [gasp]
6. A: Yeah, I know. I’m calling a furniture mover before anyone tries to wrestle that up the stairs.

**Version 2** — [grunt] as strong unwillingness

5. B: [grunt]
6. A: I know it’s a huge pain, but it’s free and would clear our counter—can we at least check the stairwell before we turn it down?

**Why the vocalization changes the response:**
The gasp calls for acknowledging the alarming weight and arranging professional help, while the grunt signals resistance that A addresses by making a case and asking B to consider the cabinet rather than immediately rejecting it.
